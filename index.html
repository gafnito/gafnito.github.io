About Me

I am a postdoctoral researcher in the Department of Industrial Engineering and Operations Research (IEOR) at Columbia University, working with Professors Garud Iyengar and Assaf Zeevi.

I received my Ph.D. in Electrical and Computer Engineering from Ben-Gurion University of the Negev in 2024, under the supervision of Professor Kobi Cohen. Prior to that, I received my M.Sc. and B.Sc. degrees from Ben-Gurion University in 2019 and 2018, respectively.

My research lies at the intersection of information theory, statistics, and machine learning. I develop theory and algorithms for sequential decision-making under uncertainty and non-stationarity.

Before joining Columbia, I worked on quantization algorithms for large language models at Intel and machine learning for communications at Elbit Systems.

My detailed CV can be found here.

Research Interests

  • Online learning in non-stationary environments.
  • Sequential decision-making and multi-armed bandits.
  • Active hypothesis testing and anomaly search.
  • Change detection and statistical inference.
  • Federated learning and wireless communications.
  • Efficient quantization for deep neural networks.
